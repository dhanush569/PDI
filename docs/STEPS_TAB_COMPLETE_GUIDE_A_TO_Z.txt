1. OVERVIEW & WORKSPACE LAYOUT
================================================================================
The "Steps" tab is the sequence and process authoring center of the station.
Here, the Manufacturing/Quality Engineer defines the exact step-by-step
standard operating procedure (SOP), linking operator physical actions, 3D
depth sensor verifications, vision quality checks, projector visual guides,
and touchscreen confirmations.

The screen is divided into three primary functional areas:
  • Left Pane: Step Tree Hierarchy (drag-and-drop tree structure)
  • Center Pane: Visual Simulation & Live Projection/Screen Preview
  • Right Pane: Properties, Visualizations, Translations, & Variant Matrix


================================================================================
2. TOP TOOLBAR & CONTROLS (A TO Z)
================================================================================
Across the top of the Steps page:

[+ New Step ▼]
  Opens the complete Step Library flyout with a searchable category panel.
  Allows adding any step type directly below the currently selected node.

[Indent  (->)]
  Nests the selected step one level deeper into the step directly above it
  (e.g., turning a Place step into a child of a Numbered or Composite step).

[Outdent (<-)]
  Promotes a child step up one hierarchical level out of its parent.

[Move Up (↑) / Move Down (↓)]
  Reorders execution sequences within the current hierarchy branch.

[Delete (Trash)]
  Deletes the selected step and any nested child steps inside it.

[Search for Steps [  ] ▲ ▼]
  Live interactive text filter. Type any keyword to highlight matching steps
  across deep, multi-level tree branches.

[Expand All / Collapse All]
  Instantly unfolds the entire tree to review all child substeps, or collapses
  down to the top-level Numbered milestones.

[Columns Toggle: Variants | Instructions | None]
  • Variants: Displays variant assignment checkboxes per step.
  • Instructions: Displays an inline preview of operator guidance text.
  • None: Compact view displaying only step icons and names.

[Display Preview Switch: Projector | Screen]
  • Projector: Shows what is optically projected down onto the table/part.
  • Screen: Shows what appears on the operator's touch monitor.

3. COMPLETE STEP LIBRARY BY CATEGORY
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 CATEGORY 1: GENERAL (ALL 12 STEPS SHOWN IN CATALOG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NUMBERED STEP
   • Purpose: Represents a high-level logical assembly milestone from the
     viewpoint of the human operator (e.g., "1. Fix Cap", "2. Tighten Bolts").
   • Structure: Acts as a container. Granular detection and validation steps
     are nested underneath.
   • UI Display: Automatically populates the "Numbered Steps List" (table of
     contents) on the operator touchscreen, highlighting current progress.

2. COMPOSITE STEP
   • Purpose: Groups two or more steps together to enforce complex flow rules,
     parallel execution, concurrency, or loop repetitions.
   • Modes:
     - Sequential: Runs children one-by-one in strict order (default).
     - Any Order: Steps can be done in any sequence, but all must complete.
     - One Arbitrary: Completing any single child satisfies the step.
     - All Simultaneously: All child steps must be completed at the exact same
       millisecond (e.g., two-hand safety confirmation).

3. DELAY
   • Purpose: Pauses execution for a specified duration in milliseconds.
   • Use Case: Waiting 1500 ms for adhesive curing, settling time after press-fit,
     or allowing a mechanical clamp to stabilize.

4. EXECUTE TASK
   • Purpose: Reusable process block. Calls a standardized routine defined in
     the Tasks repository, preventing duplicate programming across jobs.

5. JOB CONTROL
   • Purpose: Programmatic flow control. Directs the active engine state.
   • Capabilities:
     - Jump directly to a specific target step by name or ID.
     - Abort the running job and return to idle.
     - Advance immediately to the next step.
     - Return to the previous detection/instruction step for rework.

6. OPERATOR DIALOG
   • Purpose: Displays an interactive decision modal on the touch screen.
   • Configuration: Custom question prompt with multiple buttons
     (e.g., "Visual Inspection: Pass / Fail", or "Confirm Lot Size").
   • Storage: Stores operator answer into a designated response variable.

7. PROCESS CONTROL
   • Purpose: Starts, pauses, resets, restarts, or terminates background
     asynchronous station processes (e.g., scanner daemon or PLC monitor).

8. RESETTER
   • Purpose: Resets variables, counters, virtual buttons, or detection flags
     back to their initial default states prior to a new cycle.

9. SNAPSHOT
   • Purpose: Captures a photo from the Color RGB, 3D Depth, or Infrared camera.
   • Storage: Embeds full image data into PostgreSQL / filesystem for audit
     traceability and customer compliance reports.

10. SWITCH JOB OPTION
    • Purpose: Structural variant branch. Evaluates the job option (e.g.,
      Model = Standard vs Luxury vs Export) and creates parallel tree paths.
    • Merging: Identical branches can be merged to avoid duplicated steps.

11. SWITCH STEP
    • Purpose: Dynamic data-driven branching. Evaluates a runtime variable
      (e.g., Torque_Result, Barcode_Prefix) and routes to matching sub-cases.
    • Fallback: Includes a "Default Case" if no specific value matches.

12. WORK INSTRUCTION
    • Purpose: Displays explanatory instructions, CAD schematics, or video aids.
    • Hold Condition: The system remains on this step until confirmed by a
      touchscreen button or sensor input.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 CATEGORY 2: VARIABLES (DATA PROCESSING & LOGIC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. COUNTER
   • Increments or decrements an integer variable by 1 (e.g., part counters).

2. FIND AND REPLACE STRING
   • Searches for a target substring and replaces it with another string
     (e.g., stripping carriage returns \r\n from raw scanner inputs).

3. FUNCTION
   • Evaluates arithmetic calculations and logical expressions using variables
     and constants (e.g., "(Length_mm - Baseline_mm) * ScaleFactor").

4. SET VARIABLE
   • Assigns a static value or dynamic expression ({{Variable}}) to a variable.
   • Supports dynamic variable names: "Status_{{Index}}".

5. SPLIT STRING BY INDEX
   • Extracts a substring based on start index and character length (e.g.,
     extracting the manufacture year from barcode characters 3 to 6).

6. SPLIT STRING BY ARGUMENT
   • Splits a string using a delimiter (e.g., ';' or '|') and extracts the Nth
     token while storing the remainder.

7. STRING FORMATTER
   • Concatenates text and variable tokens into a unified output string
     (e.g., "PART: {{PartNo}} | LOT: {{LotId}} | OPERATOR: {{User}}").

8. STRING PARSER
   • Parses incoming JSON, XML, or serial telegrams from MES / PLC, extracting
     values into matching project variables.

9. VARIABLE CHANGED
   • Waits until a specific variable updates, reaches a target value, or
     matches/differs from another variable.

10. VARIABLE LOGGER
    • Records the current snapshot of specified variables into the traceability
      database and audit trail.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 CATEGORY 3: DETECTIONS (3D SENSOR & VISION CHECKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. OBJECT — PLACE
   • Validates that the specified physical part enters and remains settled
     within its calibrated 3D bounding box on the workbench.

2. OBJECT — TAKE
   • Validates that the specified physical part is lifted or removed from
     its calibrated zone on the workbench.

3. TOOL — TAKE / PLACE
   • Tracks physical hand tools (screwdrivers, torque wrenches, grease guns).
   • Verifies tool is taken before assembly and returned to holster after.

4. PICKING BIN — PICK
   • Senses the operator's hand entering the assigned picking bin opening.
   • Validates part retrieval from the correct container.

5. ACTIVITY
   • Validates dynamic human hand gestures or assembly operations within a zone
     (e.g., pressing a seal, inserting a wiring clip, twisting a dial).
   • Supports repetition counts (e.g., requires 3 consecutive actions).

6. VIRTUAL BUTTON
   • Projects a virtual light button onto the workbench surface.
   • Completes when the operator's hand hovers over / touches the button.

7. QUALITY CHECK (VISION INSPECTION)
   • Executes an optical classification algorithm on the region of interest
     (Contour, Color histogram, Height profile, Template matching, OCR).
   • Automatically generates Rework branches:
     - CORRECT: Continues forward.
     - WRONG: Triggers warning, error projection, and corrective steps.
     - MISSING: Demands part insertion before advancing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 CATEGORY 4: MATERIALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PICK MATERIAL FROM BIN
   • Links the bill-of-materials (BOM) item directly to its picking bin.
   • "Number of Items": Automatically appends quantity to instructions
     (e.g., "Pick 4 x M6 Flange Bolts").
   • Decrements inventory counters upon valid pick.

2. DYNAMIC MATERIAL STEP
   • Uses a variable ({{Material_Code}}) to dynamically select the picking bin,
     enabling flexible multi-model kitting lines.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 CATEGORY 5: SCREEN BUTTONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PRESS SCREEN BUTTON
   • Halts execution until the operator taps a specific on-screen UI button
     (e.g., "Confirm", "Next", "Rework Complete", or "Call Supervisor").


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 CATEGORY 6: COMMUNICATION (HARDWARE & NETWORKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WAIT FOR BARCODE / SCAN
   • Waits for scanner trigger (USB HID wedge or RS-232 serial COM).
   • Validates against regex and stores serial string in BARCODE variable.
   • Automatically matches and activates the corresponding job variant.

2. WAIT FOR COMMUNICATION
   • Pauses until a telegram arrives from PLC, torque controller, or MES
     (e.g., OpenProtocol torque angle/torque OK signal).

3. SEND COMMUNICATION
   • Emits serial strings, TCP packets, or digital I/O signals to external
     automation (e.g., releasing pneumatic pallet clamps upon job PASS).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7 CATEGORY 7: EXTRA (FILE & SYSTEM UTILITIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FILE MOVER
   • Moves or renames local files (e.g., archiving completed inspection logs).

2. READ FROM FILE
   • Loads configuration text or parameters from a local file into a variable.

3. SOUND
   • Plays an audible .WAV alert through workstation speakers (e.g., pleasant
     chime on step completion, loud buzzer on quality defect).

4. WRITE TO FILE
   • Appends traceability records, measurements, and timestamps to a local
     CSV or text file on disk.

4. STEP EXECUTION LOGIC & CONCURRENCY (COMPOSITE RULES)
================================================================================
Composite steps manage how multiple child operations execute:

1. SEQUENTIAL (Default)
   • Step 1 must finish before Step 2 activates, followed by Step 3.

2. ANY ORDER (Validate / Vision License)
   • All child steps activate concurrently on table/screen.
   • Operator can perform them in any sequence (e.g., tightening 4 perimeter
     screws in any order). Advances only when all are satisfied.

3. ONE ARBITRARY STEP
   • Multiple alternate steps are presented. As soon as ANY single child step
     is completed, the composite finishes immediately (e.g., confirm via
     virtual table button OR confirm via touchscreen button).

4. ALL SIMULTANEOUSLY (Safety & Dual-Hand)
   • Requires all child steps to be triggered within the same timeframe
     (e.g., two-hand palm buttons pressed concurrently for safety, or
     verifying both tools are resting in their holders simultaneously).

5. LOOPS & REPETITIONS
   • Repeat N Times: Executes child block for a fixed number of cycles.
   • Repeat for Each Element: Iterates through elements in a delimited string
     or JSON list, providing an active {{Index}} and {{Element}} variable.

5. REWORK FLOWS & QUALITY CHECK LOOPS
================================================================================
When adding a Quality Check (Vision) step, the system creates a resilient
looping architecture:

   [Quality Check Step]
            │
            ├──► Evaluates Camera Image against Taught Baseline
            │
            ├──► If CORRECT ──► Green projection ──► Advances to Next Step
            │
            └──► If WRONG / MISSING:
                    │
                    ├──► Highlights defect in bright RED on table
                    ├──► Plays error sound & sounds buzzer
                    ├──► Executes corrective sub-steps (e.g., "Remove bad part")
                    └──► Automatically LOOPS back to Quality Check
                         until part is repaired and verified CORRECT.

6. PROPERTIES PANE & VISUALIZATION MAPPING
================================================================================
Selecting any step opens its properties on the right:

1. Header & Identity
   • Step Name: Human-readable title in the editor tree.
   • Object ID: Unique programmatic GUID for API and reporting queries.

2. Operator Instruction
   • Primary Text: Projected on workbench and shown on screen banner.
   • Multi-language: Language translations for diverse workforces.
   • Separator Word: Auto-generates combined text (e.g., "Pick Bolt AND Washer").

3. Projector Visualizations
   • Highlight Color: Green (#00E676), Blue (#2979FF), Amber (#FF9100), Red (#FF1744).
   • Shapes: Projected rectangle, circle, contour mask, or animated arrow.
   • Anchor: Locks to detection zone coordinates.

4. Screen Visualizations
   • Auxiliary Media: Displays assembly CAD, photo reference, or video tutorial.
   • Progress Bar & Numbered Steps List widget.

5. Variant Filter Matrix
   • Checkbox grid: Controls whether the step applies to Variant A, Variant B,
     or All Variants.

6. Timing & Timeouts
   • Minimum Dwell Time (ms): Ensures operator holds part stable before validation.
   • Warning Timeout (s): Turns visual banner amber if step takes unusually long.

7. PRACTICAL EXAMPLE: RACK & PINION ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE
================================================================================
Here is how these steps combine into a complete production inspection job:

1. [General: Numbered Step] "1. Component Verification & Scanning"
   ├── [Communication: Wait for Barcode] "Scan Housing QR Code" (Selects Variant)
   └── [General: Snapshot] "Record Housing Serial Photo"

2. [General: Numbered Step] "2. Bushing Insertion & Seat Check"
   ├── [Materials: Pick Material] "Pick Delrin Bushing from Bin 1"
   ├── [Detections: Object Place] "Place Bushing into Pinion Bore"
   ├── [Detections: Activity] "Press Bushing Firmly into Seat"
   └── [Detections: Quality Check] "Verify Bushing Depth & Seating"
         ├── If CORRECT: Proceeds
         └── If WRONG: "Re-seat Bushing" (Loops until verified OK)

3. [General: Numbered Step] "3. Rack Bar Placement & Meshing"
   ├── [Composite Step: Any Order] "Pick Rack Bar & Apply Grease"
   │     ├── [Materials: Pick Material] "Take Rack Bar from Shelf"
   │     └── [Detections: Activity] "Apply Synthetic Grease"
   └── [Detections: Object Place] "Place Rack Bar into Main Casting"

4. [General: Numbered Step] "4. Final Inspection & Sign-off"
   ├── [General: Snapshot] "Capture Completed PDI Image"
   ├── [General: Operator Dialog] "Confirm Smooth Rotation Without Play"
   └── [Communication: Send Communication] "Send PASS Telegram to Line PLC"
